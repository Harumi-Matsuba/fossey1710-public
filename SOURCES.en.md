# Sources

## The manuscript

Beinecke Rare Book and Manuscript Library, Yale University, **GEN MSS VOL 855**,
"Notebook of veterinary recipes : manuscript", 1710–1716.

- Catalogue record: https://collections.library.yale.edu/catalog/33569870
- IIIF manifest: https://collections.library.yale.edu/manifests/33569870
- 76 images, fully digitised, public access.

The owner signs himself **Thomas Fossey** (image 5, "Thomas Fossey 1716"); several
recipes end "Approved by me Tho: Fossey" or "Ed. Fossey approved". The flyleaves also
carry the names William Edward and William Feniel.

Images are cited here by the image number of the IIIF manifest (1–76), because the
notebook's own pagination is partial. The catalogue record gives each image a label
("p. 1 [recto]" and so on); those labels are repeated in `data/pages.tsv` when a page
is read.

No image files are reproduced in this repository. Every image can be fetched from the
manifest above; the exact request used here is

    https://collections.library.yale.edu/iiif/2/<image id>/full/<width>,/0/default.jpg

## The catalogue entry this work answers

Daniel Bourdeau's catalogue of unsolved historical ciphers, entry **818**:
"Thomas Fossey's notebook of veterinary recipes, sections in a vowel-to-digit cipher".
https://github.com/dbourdeau/cyphersolver

The entry records the Beinecke catalogue note "Some sections are completed in a
cryptographic code", states that the item is not on DECODE, and marks its prior art
"open (checked 28 Oct 2026)".

## Related

The Beinecke catalogue does not print a decipherment, and none is written in the
notebook itself.
