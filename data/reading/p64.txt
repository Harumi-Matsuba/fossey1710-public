A Drink for a Horse
that is gressed Heelled to
purge his Blood.
one ounce of flouuer
of Brimstone one
ounce of Termerick
halfe a Pound of
Trick.ell put it in a
pint of Chamber lye
and boyle a little handfull
of Rue in a quart of
Chamber lye till it comes
to a pint or less when cold
mix it together and give y{e}
