A Drink for a Cow {Drye} Gargete
Take a handfull of rue a
handfull of fetherfue halfe
a Quarton of a pound of
fenel Seed Boyle them in
a quart of Ale when it is blood
warme put in one ounce of
Termerick and halfe a
pound of Srackle fast her
{Blood her in y{e} Tayle}
two or three Ouers then give
it her blood warme shut her
up all knight in y{e} Morning
Give her Worme Warter to
Drink miss two days and give
her again and so continue till
she is well this is for y{e} Drye garget
Approved by me
Squere Tho: Fossey
