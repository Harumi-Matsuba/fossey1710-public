For a Horse that is greesed
Heelled.
Purge him well three of
four times And Rowell him then
take some old Stale & strane it
thrughe some [del: ...] a shees three
or four timee then wash his
heells weell with it every day &
as soone as it is drye take some
sharpe warter made with a pint
of Spring uuarter & tuuo ounces
of Roman uittery. if it is watrey
Cut them off and do it y{e} stuff a
boue mentioned.
