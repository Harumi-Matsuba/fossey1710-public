for a Horse that is Greesed Heelled
y{e} first place rouuell him
& then [del: giue] bout two days after
give him a Purge then miss two
days then give him another Purge
& about four or fiue days after give
him another Purge then as soone
as it has done working wash his heells
well with Beef [del: ...] salt Brine
then y{e} next day wash it again with
Beef salt Brine then as soone
as it is drye do it with some sharpe
warter made a week or more before
hand with a quart of Cold trof
warter and a handfull of Cole
sutt warme y{e} uuarter and put y{e}
