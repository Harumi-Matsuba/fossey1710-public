For a Couu that has a sore
a sore Bagg: Houuelick
Plantern eldern wormwood
Camayile Berdocks leaues
a Derstongue Mollowes Smalage
Stinking hemlock sreua
them and Boyl them in
Seay tell y{e} uerty gane
then strain it and anoint
y{e} Bagg where it is sore
make it in May.
