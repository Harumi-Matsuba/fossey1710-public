For a Horse that Is taken
Suddenly With a great Cold and
fallen into his feet and Lims —
take a Ounce of Elecompain
halfe a [del: ounce] ounce of Termerick
an ounce of lickerish an ounce
of anneseeds two ounces of
flouuer a brimstone and some
Tracle give y{e} horse [del: it] some of
it twice a day in some Warme
Ale and wash his sinewes of his
fore leggs with some good Old Verges
and some Whitt Wine Vinegar and
some Bole Americk mingled well
to geather and [del: put] take his Shues
off and draw y{e} Sole of his feet as
thin as you cane but be suer you do
not make him bleed and then dress
his feet with tar and a little fresh
suene & a little turpetine [del: ming]
mingled together and put come too
into it and tie it on with some
Rayys
