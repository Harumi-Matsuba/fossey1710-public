How to make y{e} powder of
Honey & lime.
Take such a quantity of
unsleckt lime as you shall
think fit beat it into
uery fine pouder: then
take so much honey as
shall sufice to mingle
it together & make it into
a uery stif past in y{e}
forme of a thick cake or loaf
then put y{e} same cake or loaf
into a hot Oven or a buring
fier till it be baked or burnt
glowing red then take it
forth & when it is cold beat
it in very fine powder & then
vse it as Occasion shall serve
Ed. Fossey approved
