For a Horse that
swells at heells or Runs
a little or both it will
clenge his Blood & cuer
his heell most marvelosly
Take as much Crocuumetel
ogum as will lye on a shill-
ing when beat to bowder & a
Little Elecompain & a little
Lickrish pouuder & a little
Diapentue mixt these things
with a little honey till you
make a ball of it then give
y{e} horse it in a moring farting
& let him fast e howers but
it may be gave to y{e} horse
about three howers before he
shuts out from plow & when
he comes home give him some
scalded Brane and then let him
feed & drink as vsual.
