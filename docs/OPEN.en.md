## Doubts about the reading

- **Digits against letters.** In this hand the digit 1 and the long s, and 9 and the
  long s, look alike. On image 19 "B4?6?9729y" is most likely "Bolearmery" (Bole
  Armeniac; written plainly as "bole armery" on image 24 of the same notebook), but the
  third and fifth characters cannot be settled from the image alone.
- The absence of **0** has been checked, but on images up to 1600 px wide, not on
  full-size images of every page.

  not read.
- The run of digits on image 6 ("3182 849933 / 55188sh 5526ch …") does not give words

  signature Fossey, depending on whether the long s is taken as a letter or a digit.
